            How this site works

            Match facts

            Every result, venue, lineup, official and scoring event comes from Wikipedia and is attributed on the page that shows it, with a link to the exact article, the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, and a note that we parsed and normalised it from wikitext. Nothing is inferred. Where the source has no value, the page says not recorded rather than showing a blank.

            The four honesty states

            A fact is either present, absent from the source, not yet fetched, or its last fetch failed — and the four look different on purpose:

            • Present: plain type.
            • Absent from the source: not recorded — calm, because a gap in 1891’s records is not our fault.
            • Not yet fetched: plus history still loading.
            • Fetch failed: ⚠ temporarily unavailable — alarmed, because something is actually wrong.

            How the derived figures are computed

            Four figures on this site are arithmetic we do over facts already shown here. They are not inferences, so they carry a plain count caption stating the denominator rather than the “computed” stamp — that stamp is reserved for genuine inference.

            • Form — last five tests: the five most recent completed tests. A test with no recorded result is shown as such and excluded from the tally.
            • The record by era: wins as a share of the tests with a recorded result, in four era buckets. Tests without a recorded result are hatched and excluded from the percentage.
            • Head-to-head: played, won, lost and drawn against one opponent, plus the biggest win and defeat among meetings where both scores are recorded.
            • Score progression: a running total built from the timed scoring events using the points values of the era. It is drawn only when every scoring event has a recorded minute and the running total reconciles exactly to the final score. If it does not reconcile, no chart is drawn and the page says so — we would rather show nothing than a chart that disagrees with the score. Matches before 1894 are never charted, because the points values changed three times between 1890 and 1894 and we will not encode values we cannot verify.

            Fan mood (computed)

            Fan mood is the one thing on this site we infer. It is a lexicon score over a match thread’s comments — or, where no thread exists, over news headlines — bucketed pre-match / first half / second half / post-match wherever a kickoff time exists. It always carries the computed stamp, a link to this page, and a link to what it was computed from. It is never phrased as anyone else’s opinion. Under 25 comments we show no number at all.

            What we do not do

            No live scores. No predictions. No standings. No bulk download. No accounts.
